CampusDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

CampusDesk is school management software. Schools use it to manage students, fees, exams and results, attendance, staff and related records. This policy covers the CampusDesk website and the three CampusDesk Android apps:

  • CampusDesk, for parents (com.campusdesk.parent)
  • CampusDesk Staff, for teachers and school staff (com.campusdesk.staff)
  • CampusDesk Admin, for school owners (com.campusdesk.admin)

In this policy, "we" means the CampusDesk team, and "your school" means the school that gave you your account.

Who controls your data

Your school decides what information is entered into CampusDesk and who can see it. We process that information on the school's behalf, only to provide the service. If you have a question about a record, such as a mark, a fee or a personal detail, contact your school first. You can also contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

Accounts

You cannot sign up in the apps. Your school creates your account and gives you your login. Each app only lets in the people it is meant for: parents use CampusDesk, staff use CampusDesk Staff, and school owners use CampusDesk Admin.

Information we hold

Depending on your role and what your school records, CampusDesk may hold:

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, role, and your password stored in hashed form. We cannot read your password.
  • Student records: name, scholar or admission number, class and section, date of birth, parents' or guardians' names and contact details, address, photo, and other details your school records, such as health information and documents.
  • Academic records: exams, marks, grades, report cards, attendance and homework.
  • Fee records: fee structures, amounts due, payments recorded by the school, receipts and dues. The apps do not take card or bank payments and do not store card details.
  • Staff records (staff and owner apps only): staff details and, where the school uses it, payroll information.

What the apps collect from your phone

  • The apps do not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone storage. The only exception is saving a file, such as a report card or receipt, when you choose to download or share it.
  • Your login session is stored in the phone's encrypted secure storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • The apps contain no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

How we use information

We use information only to run CampusDesk for your school:

  • to sign you in;
  • to show you the records your role allows, for example a parent sees only their own children;
  • to send service emails such as password resets.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run CampusDesk, and only as needed:

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the servers and database in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region in India.
  • An email delivery provider sends service emails such as password resets.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

All traffic between the apps or website and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Access is limited by role, and each school's data is kept separate from other schools' data. Passwords are stored only as hashes.

Retention and deletion

School records are kept for as long as your school uses CampusDesk, or as long as your school needs them. When a school stops using CampusDesk, its data is deleted on request.

To delete your account or personal data:

  • ask your school; or
  • email info@freedomworldschool.com with the subject "CampusDesk data deletion", your name and your school.

We will confirm the request with your school and act on it within 30 days. Some records, such as fee receipts, may need to be kept by the school for legal or accounting reasons.

Children

CampusDesk holds information about students because their school records it. Children do not use the apps or create accounts. The apps are used by parents, guardians and school staff.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
